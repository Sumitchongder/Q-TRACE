# Hardware experiment

## Protocol

* **Links.** Up to 40 (data, ancilla) qubit pairs, ranked by two-qubit-gate plus readout error and
  mutually non-adjacent (no coupler between qubits of different links).
* **Settings.** Each circuit assigns link `i` the setting `(a, α, β) = SETTINGS[(i + c) mod 8]`: Alice
  prepares `|a⟩` (Z) or `H|a⟩` (X); Bob rotates by `H` for the X basis and measures.
* **Epochs.** Two commissioning passes (all honest) followed by six monitoring epochs of one pass
  each. Every link is honest in two monitoring epochs and attacked in four, with distinct probes; probe
  usage is balanced (seeded schedule, `SEED = 2026`).
* **Probes.** `probe(θ)`: `RZX(θ)` or `RXX(θ)` (random per link and circuit) with Eve's ancilla measured
  in the Y basis, θ ∈ {0.15, 0.25, 0.40, 0.60, 0.90, π/2}; `ad(γ)`: `CRY(2 asin √γ)` + `CX`, γ ∈ {0.03,
  0.06, 0.12, 0.25}. Barriers separate preparation, Eve, and measurement, so all data qubits idle for the
  same duration within a circuit. No error mitigation, twirling or dynamical decoupling is applied: the
  detector must work on raw device statistics, and commissioning absorbs static readout bias.
* **Budget.** A pilot job (8 circuits × 400 shots) measures quantum seconds per shot; the main job (64
  circuits) uses the largest multiple of 100 shots (1000–8000) that keeps pilot + main below
  `BUDGET_SECONDS`.

## Analysis

* **Q-TRACE:** two-sample G-test over the eight settings of a link-epoch against the link's
  commissioning data, `G/φ ~ χ²_8`, with dispersion `φ` from the two commissioning passes.
* **QBER (pooled):** one-sided two-proportion test on the sifted QBER.
* **QBER (per basis):** one-sided tests on the Z and X QBERs, Bonferroni-combined.
* Detection versus shots per circuit (subsampling), noncentral-χ² prediction from the measured tables,
  Eve's mutual information (Miller–Madow corrected) on rounds where her probe basis matched Alice's, and
  the bits of sifted key leaked before the median first alarm.

## Running

1. `make rehearsal` validates the pipeline offline on FakeMarrakesh.
2. Open `notebooks/01_qpu_hardware_run.ipynb`, run Sections 0–2, check the selected links.
3. Section 3 with `CONFIRM = True` (pilot), review the plan, Section 4 with `RUN_MAIN = True`.
4. Section 5 analyses the data locally. Send back `results/hardware/hardware_<backend>_<time>.npz`
   and the CSVs next to it.

## Interpretation notes

The hardware emulates a QKD channel with superconducting qubits: "loss" and multi-photon effects are
absent, and the honest noise is gate, idle and readout error. The experiment tests the parts of Q-TRACE
that do not depend on optical physics (calibration against a device's own commissioning data,
false-alarm control under real drift, the advantage of full-table tests over QBER monitoring, and the
agreement of detection rates with information-theoretic predictions) on a device whose noise is not
generated by the detector's model.

## Results of the ibm_marrakesh run (2026-11-21)

* 33 links passed the quality cut-offs; 64 circuits x 5900 shots; 107 QPU seconds in total
  (3 s pilot, 104 s main job).
* **Pre-registered analysis (commissioning reference).** All tests, including the pooled and
  per-basis QBER monitors, flagged about 92 % of honest link-epochs. The honest monitoring epochs
  show a QBER increase of +1.86 % in the X basis and +0.32 % in the Z basis relative to
  commissioning, with no trend over time (the two commissioning passes agree to 1e-4). Commissioning
  circuits contained no probe gates, so their qubits were measured immediately, whereas monitoring
  circuits are barrier-aligned to the longest probe and run concurrently with two-qubit gates
  elsewhere on the chip. The detectors were right: the operating conditions had changed.
* **Condition-matched analysis (post hoc).** Using each link's honest monitoring epochs as
  reference, honest false alarms fall to 0/66, the measured QBER increase of every probe agrees with
  the ideal-device prediction, and the weakest probe (theta = 0.15, ideal QBER increase 0.28 %) is
  detected on 14/14 link-epochs by Q-TRACE, 13/14 by the per-basis QBER test and 9/14 by the pooled
  QBER test.
* **Lesson.** Reference statistics for any telemetry monitor must be acquired under the same
  operating conditions as the monitored traffic. A follow-up run should insert timing-matched
  delays (and identical concurrent gate activity) into the commissioning circuits.
